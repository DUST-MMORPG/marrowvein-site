The game

An original online RPG, built by one developer

Marrowvein is an original online RPG (MMO) in development by a solo developer, inspired by classic sandbox online RPGs.

It's one persistent world shared by every player. Fights are full-loot, and the world keeps track of what you do: crimes can have witnesses, the guilty get bounties, and your notoriety follows you. The look is gritty and muted, cut through by bright flashes of magic.

  • GenreOnline RPG (MMO)
  • WorldPersistent, shared, dedicated server

Features

What's in the world

Marrowvein is still in pre-alpha, so this is the game as it's being built.

  • No pay-to-win. Ever.

    Power is earned in the world, never bought. No loot boxes, no random paid rewards. If we ever sell anything, it will be cosmetics you choose directly, and nothing that affects a fight.

  • A persistent shared world

    One world, running on a dedicated server, shared by everyone who plays.

  • Full-loot PvP and notoriety

    Anyone can be attacked anywhere. Attack or kill the innocent, though, and you're branded a criminal or murderer, with a bounty on your head for anyone to hunt.

  • Crime, witnesses and bounties

    Crimes can be seen by witnesses. Track down outlaws and bring back their heads for the reward.

  • Looting and stealing

    Gear and items can be looted and stolen.

  • Action combat

    Third-person action combat, with magic in the mix.

  • Skills, not classes

    Grow by doing. Every skill sharpens through use, from blade and spell to stealth and the reading of old maps, and no one can master everything.

  • Hired adventurers

    Hire adventurers who follow you and fight at your side.

  • Gems

    Gems are the currency of value.

  • Risk vs. reward

    What you carry is what you can lose. Venture further, carry more gems and fight for better loot, but everything on you is at stake.

  • Your name means something

    Every deed follows you. Help the innocent or prey on them, and the realm remembers, from honored champion to feared tyrant.

  • The Wardens

    Each city has its keepers. Bring outlaws to justice and earn your place among them, or become the one they hunt.

  • Gems with purpose

    The vein runs through everything. The gems you pry from the dark don't just buy things, they make your gear stronger.

And this is only the beginning. Much more is already planned, and the world will keep growing with every update.
