The game

An original online RPG, built by one developer

Marrowvein is an original online RPG (MMO) in development by a solo developer, inspired by classic sandbox online RPGs.

Fights are full-loot, and the world keeps track of what you do: crimes can have witnesses, the guilty get bounties, and your notoriety follows you. The look is gritty and muted, cut through by bright flashes of magic.

  • GenreOnline RPG (MMO)

Features

What's in the world

Marrowvein is still in pre-alpha, so this is the game as it's being built.

No pay-to-win. Ever.

Power is earned in the world, never bought. No loot boxes, no random paid rewards. If we ever sell anything, it will be cosmetics you choose directly, and nothing that affects a fight.

Adventuring

  • No classes

    You become what you practice. Steel, spell or shadow, and the path is yours to carve.

  • Risk vs. reward

    What you carry is what you can lose. Venture further, carry more gems and fight for better loot, but everything on you is at stake. Insure the gear you can't bear to lose.

  • Gems

    Gems are the currency of value. The vein runs through everything. The gems you pry from the dark don't just buy things, they make your gear stronger.

  • Creatures and monsters

    Beasts stalk the wilds. Undead haunt the crypts. Deeper still, things far worse wait in the dark.

  • Crafting

    Crafting is simplified, not a grind.

Reputation

  • Crime, witnesses and bounties

    Crimes can be seen by witnesses. Track down outlaws and bring back their heads for the reward.

  • Looting and stealing

    Gear and items can be looted and stolen.

  • A legacy written in blood and honor

    Every choice you make is etched into your legacy, and the world never forgets. Become a hero people whisper about, or a name they fear.

PvP

  • Full-loot PvP and notoriety

    Anyone can be attacked anywhere. Attack or kill the innocent, though, and you're branded a criminal or an outlaw, with a bounty on your head for anyone to hunt.

  • Action combat

    Third-person action combat, with magic in the mix.

  • Two schools of magic

    Wizardry bends raw force. Necromancy commands the dead: summon horrors, raise the fallen, and send them back at your enemies.

  • The Wardens

    Each city has its keepers. Bring outlaws to justice and earn your place among them, or become the one they hunt.

And this is only the beginning. Much more is already planned, and the world will keep growing with every update.
