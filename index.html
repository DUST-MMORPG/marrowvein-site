The game

An original online RPG, built by one developer

Marrowvein is an original online RPG (MMO) in development by a solo developer in Texas, inspired by classic sandbox online RPGs.

It's one persistent world shared by every player. Fights are full-loot, and the world keeps track of what you do: crimes can have witnesses, the guilty get bounties, and your notoriety follows you. The look is dark and muted, lit by bright flashes of magic.

  • GenreOnline RPG (MMO)
  • WorldPersistent, shared, dedicated server
  • PvPFull loot, with notoriety
  • DeveloperSolo developer, Texas
  • StatusPre-alpha. Paid alpha planned

Features

What's in the world

Marrowvein is still in pre-alpha, so this is the game as it's being built.

  • A persistent shared world

    One world, running on a dedicated server, shared by everyone who plays.

  • Full-loot PvP and notoriety

    Anything you carry can be taken. Your standing is tracked as innocent, criminal or murderer.

  • Crime, witnesses and bounties

    Crimes can be seen by witnesses. Bounties go on the guilty, and you can bring back their heads for rewards.

  • Looting and stealing

    Looting a corpse and stealing from someone's pack are two separate crimes.

  • Wands and staves

    Load wands and staves with spells, and keep your spells on an action bar.

  • Wizardry

    Cast spells like Energy Arc, Pyro Pillar, Deep Mend and Pyro Burst.

  • Skills with 10 levels

    Every skill has 10 levels to work through.

  • A dodge roll

    Roll out of danger in light gear. In heavy gear it becomes a side-step.

  • Day and night

    A real day/night cycle, with a huge moon, a red planet and purple moonlight.

  • Places to explore

    The town of Lanternford, a forest graveyard with catacombs below, and the Waygate.

  • Hired adventurers

    Hire adventurers who follow you and fight at your side.

  • Gems

    Gems are the currency of value.
